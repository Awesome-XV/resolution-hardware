# Fadeaway

I am building a RC circuit so that when the switch is open, the three LEDs fade out at different speeds from left to right using RC timing.

# What I did

Built a light effect circuit in Falstad using RC timing, diodes, and LEDs. I ran into a few wiring issues such as the charge resistor that was too large and that caused it to take too long to charge up.
After fixing those, the circuit worked, opening the switch makes each LED slowly light up and then each fading out at a different speed based on their position.
